    The 7:45 AM Phone Rule (From Page 14 of the JioWave Brief)

    "This is the Power BI page Rashmi opens on her phone at 7:45 AM before her first call. Build for the three-minute reader, then drill behind each card for the operations team who has more time." Six clean visuals beat sixty messy ones!

    1. F-Pattern Eye Tracking

    Executives read top-left to bottom-right. Place your Headline KPI Cards on the left/top, your Hero Trend Visual in the center, and your Action Watchlist on the right.

    2. Color Restraint (Max 3 Colors)

    Use neutral slate/navy for baseline bars, Teal/Blue for JioWave target performance, and Crimson/Amber exclusively to call attention to anomalies like the 68.43% churn cohort!

    3. Action Titles, Not Label Titles

    Never title a chart "Churn Rate by Cohort". Title it: "2024Q3 × PL015 × Tier-2 Churns at 68.4% (5.7× Headline)".

    Topic 4: KPI Card Mastery 00:30 – 00:40

    Topic 4: Building Executive KPI Cards with Reference Labels

    A naked number like `11.97%` has no context. Power BI's New Card Visual lets you attach MoM Trend, Benchmark Comparison, and Status Flags inside one unified card!

    How to Configure a Multi-Layer KPI Card
    1. Insert the Card (new) visual from the Visualizations pane.
    2. Drag all 6 core measures into the Data field well simultaneously (Power BI arranges them in a clean 1×6 vertical grid!).
    3. Go to Format Visual → Reference labels: select each KPI card and add its matching MoM Trend measure and Status Flag measure underneath!
    4. Turn on Accent bar (left edge colored Teal for healthy, Crimson for alert).
    DAX Measure for Worst Cohort Card
    Worst Cohort Churn % =
    CALCULATE(
      [Churn Rate %],
      dim_subscribers[Vintage_Cohort] = "2024Q3",
      dim_plans[plan_id] = "PL015",
      dim_towers[tower_tier] = "Tier-2"
    ) -- Evaluates to 68.43%
    Topic 5: Trend Storytelling 00:40 – 00:50

    Topic 5: Hero Line Chart + Analytics Pane Benchmark Lines

    How do we show Rashmi at a glance where JioWave's ARPU sits relative to the ₹700 challenger floor?

    Building the Center Hero Line Chart
    • X-Axis: dim_date[Date] (set to Continuous axis so all 36 months flow smoothly without horizontal scrollbars!).
    • Y-Axis: [Monthly ARPU].
    • Legend: dim_subscribers[subscriber_tier] (Diamond, Premium, Mass).
    • Turn on Series labels (so tier names sit directly at the end of each line instead of forcing the eye up to a legend!).
    Adding Benchmark Lines (Magnifying Glass 🔍 Icon)
    1. Select the visual → click the Add further analyses to your visual (🔍) tab.
    2. Expand Constant line → + Add line.
    3. Set Value = 700, Color = Dashed Gold, Data Label = "Target Premium Floor (₹700)".
    Topic 6: Interactive Filtering 00:50 – 01:00

    Topic 6: Slicers & Sync Slicers — Controlling All 5 Pages Together

    If CMO Rashmi selects `Fiscal Year = FY26` on Page 1, she expects Pages 2, 3, 4, and 5 to stay filtered to `FY26` when she clicks through!

    Modern Slicer Best Practices
    • Limit canvas slicers to 3 or 4 high-impact dimensions in the top header bar: Fiscal_Year, tower_tier (Metro / Tier-1 / Tier-2), and segment (Prepaid / Postpaid / Broadband).
    • Use Tile / Button style (`Slicer settings → Style: Tile`) for short lists like FY24 | FY25 | FY26 so the CMO can tap them on her phone!
    • Put low-frequency filters (like specific city or occupation) inside the collapsible Filters Pane on the right.
    Sync Slicers (`View → Sync slicers`)
    1. Select your Fiscal_Year slicer on Page 1.
    2. Open View Tab → Sync slicers.
    3. Check the Sync (🔄) box across Pages 1–5, and check the Visible (👁️) box wherever you want the slicer bar displayed!
    Topic 7: Visual Cues 01:00 – 01:10

    Topic 7: Conditional Formatting — Making Anomalies Jump Off the Screen

    On Page 1's Right Rail Watchlist table, we don't want Rashmi reading 20 numbers. Conditional formatting guides her eyes straight to the danger zones.

    1. Background Color Scale

    Right-click [Churn Rate %] in the table field well → Conditional formatting → Background color. Set Low (6%) = Soft Mint, High (60%+) = Crimson Red.

    2. Inline Data Bars

    Apply Data bars on [Total Subscribers] so the CMO immediately sees whether a high churn rate affects 10 customers or 849 customers!

    3. Rule-Based KPI Icons

    Apply Icons on [Churn Rate %]: Green Check if < 0.12, Amber Triangle if 0.12 – 0.25, Red Diamond if > 0.25.
