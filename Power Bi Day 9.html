    Topic 3: Interactive Storytelling 00:28 – 00:40

    Topic 3: Bookmarks & Selection Pane — Switching Board Views in 1 Click

    Remember Principle #6 on Page 11 of the JioWave Brief? "Three bookmarks — Executive Summary, Cohort Detail, Methodology. Rashmi clicks bookmarks during the board call. They must work the first time, every time."

    Step-by-Step Bookmark & Button Setup
    1. Go to View Tab → Check [✓] Bookmarks & [✓] Selection.
    2. Use the Selection Pane (eye icons 👁️) to show/hide specific visual groups (e.g., show Retire 5 Migration Table vs. 90-Day Playbook Cards).
    3. Click Add in the Bookmarks pane, rename to BM_Retire_5_Plan, click the ... menu and uncheck Data if you don't want the bookmark to reset the CMO's active slicers!
    4. Insert Insert → Buttons → Navigator → Bookmark navigator to render sleek pill tabs at the top of Page 5!
    Critical Bookmark Trap to Avoid

    If you leave Data checked on a Bookmark, clicking the bookmark wipes out whatever Fiscal_Year or City Tier filter Rashmi had selected! Always uncheck Data when building view-switcher bookmarks.
