    Commerce & Economics Analogy: Price Effect vs. Sales Mix Effect

    In corporate finance, when average revenue per unit drops, the CFO always asks two questions: (1) Mix Effect: Are customers shifting toward cheaper products? (2) Rate Effect: Are existing customers spending less on overages and add-ons? Today we build the visuals and DAX measures to separate both effects!

    Topic 2: Waterfall Chart 00:16 – 00:26

    Topic 2: Waterfall Charts — Bridging Rental, Overage & Add-on Changes

    CFOs love Waterfall charts because every bar shows positive green gains and negative red drags leading from Start to End.

    How to Build the Net Migration Waterfall on Page 3
    1. Insert the Waterfall chart visual on Page 3.
    2. Category: Drag fact_plan_migrations[direction] (Up vs. Down).
    3. Breakdown: Drag dim_plans[segment] (Prepaid, Postpaid, Broadband).
    4. Y-Axis: Drag [Net Monthly Rental Change] (SUM(fact_plan_migrations[rental_change])).
    What the Waterfall Reveals Immediately
    • Upward Migrations (1,011 events): Adds +₹6,35,750/month (avg +₹628.83 per upgrade).
    • Downward Migrations (3,717 events): Bleeds -₹9,71,950/month (avg -₹261.49 per downgrade).
    • Net Monthly Recurring Drag: +₹6,35,750 - ₹9,71,950 = -₹3,36,200/month (or -₹40.34 Lakhs/year annualized recurring bleed!).

    Topic 4: Boardroom Simulation 00:36 – 00:48

    Topic 4: Interactive What-If Sliders (`Modeling → New Parameter → Numeric Range`)

    Remember Q2c & Q2d? "Quantify the FY27 revenue impact and recommend 3 intervention plays with expected rupee preservation." Let's build a live slider for CFO Rohan Bose!

    4-Click What-If Slider Setup
    1. Go to Modeling Tab → New parameter → Numeric range.
    2. Name: ARPU Recovery Uplift (INR) | Data type: Whole number | Min: 0 | Max: 150 | Increment: 10 | Default: 50.
    3. Power BI automatically generates a disconnected table using GENERATESERIES(0, 150, 10), a helper measure [ARPU Recovery Uplift Value], and puts a sleek slider directly on Page 3!
    DAX Measure Driven by the Live Slider
    Projected FY27 Premium Uplift =
    VAR ActiveHighValSubs = 6842 -- 5,723 Premium + 1,119 Diamond in Mar 26
    VAR MonthlyUplift = [ARPU Recovery Uplift Value]
    RETURN
      ActiveHighValSubs * MonthlyUplift * 12

    When the CFO drags the slider to ₹50/month, the KPI card dynamically displays +₹41.05 Lakhs/year in FY27 revenue preserved!

    Topic 5: Dynamic Visuals 00:48 – 00:58

    Topic 5: Field Parameters — Switching Between ARPU, Total Revenue & Overage % in One Chart

    Instead of cramming 3 separate trend charts onto Page 3, a Field Parameter lets Rashmi toggle the Y-axis metric with 1 button click!

    How to Create a Field Parameter
    1. Go to Modeling Tab → New parameter → Fields.
    2. Name it Select Metric and check: [Monthly ARPU], [Total Revenue INR], and [Overage Share %].
    3. Drag Select Metric into the Y-axis of your Line Chart—Power BI adds a slicer that dynamically swaps the metric and number formatting!
    Why Field Parameters Win Capstone Grading

    Keeps Page 3 clean (under 6 visuals) while giving CFO Rohan Bose and CMO Rashmi Iyer three complete analytical views inside one single hero chart!
